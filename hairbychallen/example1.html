About Afterpay NZ merchant setup

To apply as an Afterpay merchant in New Zealand, the following are required. Click each to learn more:

NZ registered business & NZBN number + −

You need to be operating as a registered business in New Zealand — this can be a sole trader, partnership, or company.

Your NZBN (New Zealand Business Number) is a free, unique 13-digit identifier issued to every NZ business. If you don't have one, register at nzbn.govt.nz — it only takes a few minutes.

Sole traders operating under their own name may also be accepted using their IRD number as supporting documentation. Afterpay will confirm what's needed during the application.

NZ business bank account + −

Afterpay pays your settlement amounts directly to a NZ bank account, so you'll need one in your business name.

Most major NZ banks (ANZ, ASB, BNZ, Kiwibank, Westpac) offer low-cost or free business accounts for sole traders. You'll need your account number and proof of account ownership when you apply.

A personal account is not accepted — even if you're a sole trader, it needs to be a dedicated business account.

An established website showing services & contact details + −

Afterpay reviews your website to confirm you're a legitimate, active business before approving a merchant account.

They look for: your business name clearly displayed, a list of services, contact information (phone, location or email), and working pages throughout the site.

This website satisfies this requirement. It includes your name, full service list, phone number, location, and Instagram link.

A published privacy policy + −

A privacy policy tells customers how their personal information is collected, used and protected. Afterpay requires one to be published on your website.

For a small salon it can be a short, simple page. You can use a free generator at iubenda.com or termsfeed.com , or adapt the following draft:

Draft — Privacy Policy

Hair by Challen respects your privacy and treats all personal information provided to me as confidential.

I may collect personal information such as your name, phone number, email address, appointment details, service preferences and other information you choose to provide when making an enquiry or booking an appointment.

This information is used only for purposes connected with providing my services, including responding to enquiries, arranging and managing bookings, communicating with you about appointments, and providing a personalised service.

All bookings, appointment details, enquiries and related customer information are treated as confidential. I do not sell, rent or otherwise disclose your personal information to third parties except where necessary to provide a service, process a payment, comply with a legal obligation, or where you have otherwise given permission.

Information shared with me through Instagram, including booking conversations and messages, is also treated as confidential.

I take reasonable steps to keep your information secure and retain it only for as long as reasonably required for business or legal purposes.

You may request access to or correction of personal information I hold about you, or ask me to delete information where appropriate, by contacting me directly.
A cancellation & refund policy + −

Afterpay requires merchants to have a clear cancellation and refund policy so customers know what to expect if plans change.

Here's the cancellation policy for Hair by Challen:

Draft — Cancellation & Refund Policy

I require at least 24 hours notice if you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment.

Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may incur a fee of 50% of the booked service value.

No-shows will incur a fee of 20% of the booked service value.

As hair services are personalised and time is reserved exclusively for you, refunds are not offered once a service has been completed.

If you are unhappy with your result, please contact me within 48 hours and I will work with you to make it right.
Apply at merchants.afterpay.com + −

Once you have everything above in place, you can apply directly through Afterpay's merchant portal.

1. Visit merchants.afterpay.com and click Get Started.

2. Complete the application with your business details, NZBN, bank account, and website URL.

3. Afterpay will review your application — this typically takes a few business days.

4. Once approved, you'll receive merchant credentials and access to the portal, where you can set up in-store payments via QR code — no hardware or e-commerce integration required.

Do you need a website? Effectively yes. Afterpay reviews your online presence as part of the merchant application to verify your business is legitimate and active. For in-salon payments, customers simply open their Afterpay app and scan a barcode at the point of sale — no online checkout is needed. However, having this website strengthens your application significantly.
